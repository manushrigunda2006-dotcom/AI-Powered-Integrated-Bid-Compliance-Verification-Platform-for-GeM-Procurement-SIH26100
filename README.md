# AI-Powered-Integrated-Bid-Compliance-Verification-Platform-for-GeM-Procurement-SIH26100


A RegTech/GovTech platform designed to assist government procurement officers in
verifying tender RFPs and evaluating bidder compliance through an explainable,
human-in-the-loop workflow.

The system combines deterministic compliance rules, document verification,
simulated government registry adapters, bidder cross-entity checks, risk scoring,
and immutable audit trails to make procurement evaluation faster, more
transparent, and less error-prone.

Key Features:
• Tender/RFP creation and management
• Existing tender browsing and evaluation
• Bidder registration and document verification
• Automated compliance and eligibility checks
• GSTN, Udyam MSME, CPPP Debarment and other registry-style checks
• Cross-entity verification using corporate information
• Dynamic bidder risk scoring
• Evidence-based compliance matrix
• Human officer review and adjudication
• Digital signature / officer credential interface
• Audit timeline and tamper-evident decision records
• GFR Rule 151 compliance support
• Exportable compliance certificates and reports

The goal is not to replace procurement officers, but to provide an explainable
decision-support system that reduces repetitive verification work while keeping
the final decision under authorized human control.
